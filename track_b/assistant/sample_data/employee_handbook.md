# Acme Support Handbook

## Support hours

The support desk is staffed Monday through Friday from 09:00 to 17:00 Asia/Kathmandu.
Urgent production incidents can be reported at any time through the incident hotline.

## Service targets

Priority 1 incidents receive an initial response within 15 minutes. Priority 2 incidents
receive an initial response within 2 business hours. General questions receive a response
within one business day.

## Refund policy

Customers may request a full refund within 30 calendar days of purchase. A refund request
must include the order number and the email address used for the purchase. Approved refunds
normally reach the original payment method within 5 to 10 business days.

## Data handling

Agents must not ask customers to provide passwords, full payment-card numbers, or private
authentication tokens. Sensitive values accidentally shared in a ticket must be redacted.
